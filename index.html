About the School

A Montessori home in the heart of Mandli, Billawar

Tucked near the PWD (R&B) Rest House in Mandli, Tehsil Billawar, Cambridge Montessori Preschool has become the neighbourhood's trusted choice for early years education. We built our classrooms around one idea: children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and free to explore at their own pace.

From Little Hearts toddlers taking their first steps away from home, to UKG students building strong academic confidence, every stage of our programme is designed to grow with your child — blending Montessori materials, modern teaching tools, and dedicated day care.

  • Located centrally in Mandli, easily reached from across Tehsil Billawar
  • Teachers trained specifically in early-childhood Montessori practice
  • Small class sizes with individual attention for every child
